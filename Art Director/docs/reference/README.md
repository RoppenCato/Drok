# Reference boards

Public-domain pictures and the studio's own, with what each one teaches and its licence. Nothing from a living artist
or another game is ever copied in here: those are looked at and written about, not kept.

## dark-fantasy: which kind of dark? (Leonard, AD, 2026-11-20)

Made to ask Robin open question 20 of the brief. His look is "Dark fantasy" (Robin, 2026-11-19), and that word covers
very different pictures: grim and grey, storybook, inked, painted, loud. Nine old paintings and book pictures, numbered,
so he can say "more like 5 and 8, never 3". They show kinds of dark. They are not what Drok will look like.

- The board as one picture: `dark-fantasy\_board.jpg` (made with `tools\numbered.js`).
- The same as a page for his phone: `dark-fantasy\kinds-of-dark.html`, published as a private artifact,
  https://claude.ai/artifact/RNP43qsmnP3Pwy1DLNGVYP
- Where each file came from: `dark-fantasy\_fetched.json` (the Commons page, the artist, the date, the licence).

All nine are from Wikimedia Commons, each marked **Public domain** there (checked per file by
`tools\commons_fetch.py` on 2026-11-20, which refuses anything else). Kept at 800 px wide, about 2 MB together.

| No. | File | The picture | What it teaches |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `1-rackham-rhinegold.jpg` | Arthur Rackham, from "The Rhinegold and the Valkyrie", 1910 | A nervous pen line with a thin wash. Gnarled, crawling shapes. An inked look where every creature is a little ugly. |
| 2 | `2-kittelsen-forest-troll.jpg` | Theodor Kittelsen, "Skogtroll" (Forest Troll), 1906 | Pale and misty, fine marks, one glowing eye. Eerie and quiet, and the monster is the land itself. |
| 3 | `3-dore-dark-forest.jpg` | Gustave Doré, Dante's Inferno, plate 1, 1857 | Black and white engraving. Light cut out of black; grim and real. How far a picture goes with no colour. |
| 4 | `4-bilibin-baba-yaga.jpg` | Ivan Bilibin, "Baba Yaga", 1900 | An even outline with flat, chosen colour. Folk-tale dark. The hand that reads best small. |
| 5 | `5-bauer-trolls.jpg` | John Bauer, "Look at them, troll mother said", 1915 | Storybook dark: big round creatures in earth colours, and one bright thing the eye goes to. |
| 6 | `6-martin-day-of-wrath.jpg` | John Martin, "The Great Day of His Wrath", about 1851 | Fire against black, huge scale. One hot colour, everything else dark round it. |
| 7 | `7-friedrich-abbey.jpg` | Caspar David Friedrich, "Abbey in the Oakwood", about 1810 | Brown fog, bare trees, a ruin. Mood from value alone; silhouettes against a pale sky. |
| 8 | `8-vasnetsov-knight.jpg` | Viktor Vasnetsov, "Knight at the Crossroads", 1882 | A painted hero in a real world: bones in the grass, a low sun. Muted ground, the rider reads first. |
| 9 | `9-boecklin-isle-of-the-dead.jpg` | Arnold Böcklin, "Isle of the Dead", third version, 1883 | Solid painted forms: pale rock, black trees, flat water. Grave rather than scary. |

Not on the board: a Harry Clarke ink drawing (Commons would not hand the file over that night) and Peter Nicolai Arbo's
"The Wild Hunt of Odin" (fetched, left out to keep to nine). Both are public domain and can be added.
