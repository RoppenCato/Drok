# Three concepts

The procedure for anything that matters a lot. Robin picks from pictures; words do not converge (`docs\rules.md` 38
to 40). Nothing is rigged, rendered as a set or wired in before he has picked.

## 1. Decide whether it is big work

Big: the look of the game, the hero, a class character, a world map, a title screen, a main creature or boss, the
interface's hand, the first place of the game. Not big: a monster of a place in an agreed hand, a prop, an icon, a
button, a later place in an agreed hand. Those are built and shown. When unsure, one line to Robin: "this is big
enough for three concepts; I will make them unless you say go straight to it."

## 2. Make three that really differ

- Three **real** stills: the thing in the real scene at the judging size, with the game's chrome on, beside our player
  where scale matters. Not sketches, not a sheet of parts.
- Three **different** stills: a different hand, construction or idea each, not three colourways of one idea. If two
  are the same vision with a change, make a third that is not. Say in one line each what the vision is and what it
  costs (frames, files, work).
- The same scene in all three, so what differs is the vision.
- A close-up beside each where the face or the line carries the vision (the hero at three times, the line at the real
  size).
- Say which you would pick and why, in one line. Robin decides.

## 3. Show them

- One page, a big number by each, a label and one line: `sites\pick.html` from the template, copied beside the
  pictures with its JSON block filled, published as an artifact; and the pictures themselves sent into the chat
  (SendUserFile), the pick page's link with them. `tools\concepts.js` makes the same as one picture when a page is
  too much.
- Then stop. Do not start on number 1.

## 4. After the pick

- Write Robin's words and the date into `docs\direction.md` (or the asset's notes), as his decision.
- If he rejects all three: ask for references, with a board of real pictures to react to (`tools\board.js`), and ask
  for two of his own. Then three new ones from those.
- If he picks one with changes: those changes, then one still of the result before the set.
- A line in `docs\log.md`.
