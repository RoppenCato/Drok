## The answer to his question (mine)

Yes, scale reads from above. But from above nothing is tall: you cannot look up at a table. So the size has to be told
by one of three things, and each belongs to one size of hero. That is what the three stills are.

All three show the same corner of the kitchen: tiles of 10 cm in cream and aqua, a rug north of them (the next biome),
a table or a chair above. The hero is 54 units tall in all three, as today. Only his size in the house changes.

**What I would pick (mine; Robin decides):** 2. Things everybody knows, whole and bigger than he is, are what make him
look tiny from above. And the insects come in every size the game already uses, 30 to 130 units, with bosses above
that. Then 1: the house reads best, but the insects are pests, not monsters. 3 has the most wonder; it is also the one
where you can hardly see that it is a house.

## Mine in all three, for Victor, GD to know

- **Today's look carries over as it is.** The insects, the things and his gear are models in two flat tones with the
  dark line; the floor is painted and has no line; the glass is the game's of today.
- **The floor is drawn tilted, as the models are seen.** A square tile is 1 wide and 0.57 tall. The island had no
  squares and no circles on its ground, so this never showed there. A house has both: a round coin and a square tile
  must agree. Nothing changes in how he walks.
- **A table or a chair is its legs and its shadow.** The top of a leg fades out. No seat and no table top is drawn, so
  nothing hides the floor. A round table throws a round shadow, a chair a square one with round corners.
- **A thing that lies flat throws its own outline as a shadow.**
- **His gear is found things, by his size:** 1 a needle with red thread, a coin, a thimble. 2 a pin with a ball head,
  a shirt button. 3 a splinter, a lentil. Stand-ins, on the points his body already has.
- **The insects are round, with big eyes, one colour each:** ant violet blue, ladybird red, cockroach amber, spider
  purple. One pose each. The spider is in still 1 only; many people dislike them.
- **Rooms as biomes** (ideas for the idea bank, not decisions): the kitchen floor, the rug, under the bed, the bathroom
  with small tiles and drops of water, the windowsill with plant pots, the bookshelf, the cellar. A doll's house for a
  town.
