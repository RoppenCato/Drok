# icon  (Leonard, AD, 2026-11-20)

**What it is:** the app's icon: the Warrior's face in his red head cloth on a bright sea, with the game's dark line.
`icon-512.png` and `icon-192.png`.

**What changed and why:** new, to replace the two placeholder icons in the game's `client\public\`. The design is mine.

**The numbers:** 512 by 512 and 192 by 192, the whole square painted (a phone may cut it to a circle or a rounded
square); the face sits inside the middle four fifths, so it can be used as a maskable icon too.

**How the game uses it:** copy the two files over the placeholders (the README of the game says nothing else changes).

**Open:** for Robin to judge. No name is written on it.

**Not tested:** not installed on a phone; not seen on a home screen.

**Origin and licence:** made here by `blender\icon.py` (the game's own model, six times the size) and `tools\icon.js`.
Nothing from outside.

**Where to see it:** the files themselves.
