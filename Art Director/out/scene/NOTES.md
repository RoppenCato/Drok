# scene  (Leonard, AD, 2026-11-20)

**What it is:** the one thing made all the way before the set: the Warrior on the island's sand beside a sand crab,
the glass on, at 412 by 915. `beach.png` is the screen at two pixels a unit; `beach-close.png` is the Warrior and the
crab at three times, pixel for pixel.

**How it was made:** by `tools\scene.js` from the folders beside this one, the way the game draws: the ground at double
size, props and figures sorted by their feet, flat shadows, the gear on the body's points, the glass at the control
test's places. Nothing in it is painted by hand, so it shows what the deliveries give.

**Judged by the studio's check (mine):** he reads as one shape and shows his facing; the tones are chosen, one light;
the figures carry the line and the ground does not, so they win; nothing thin has gone black (the grass tufts lost
their line for that); the crab's wind-up changes its outline. Not judged: motion (see the moving page), and Robin's eye.

**Open:** the hero is small on the screen at 54. A camera zoom of 1.25 would answer it; that is the game's and Robin's call.
