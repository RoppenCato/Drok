The chest opens

It shines in the colour of its best prize and shakes, pops, and throws its loot out. A rarer prize shakes longer and bursts bigger. It plays again by itself; tap the picture to open it at once.

The picture is about twice the game's size until you press "The game's size". The light is drawn from six white pieces, coloured by the game; the coin is one coin turning. Leonard, AD.
