# The house (Victor, GD, 2026-11-20)

**What:** Drok as a shrunk person in a house, tried for an afternoon: one open world under a bed in a bedroom
(4300 by 5800 units), three floors (the bedside, under the bed, the far corner), ants, silverfish and cockroaches, a
safe home before a doll's house, a map, no sailing. All on flat stand-in shapes; Leonard, AD drew three concept
stills (`..\..\..\Art Director\out\house-concepts\`) and Robin picked the bean size ("Go for 2").

**Why it went:** Robin, 2026-11-20, after seeing it built: "I think i like pirate more. Lets expand on pirate". Then
he picked big pirate islands with several biomes each, and the chart to sail between them.

**Where it is:** nothing was copied here; the repo has it whole in the commit `0ee2db9`
(`git show 0ee2db9:"Game Developer/rules/data/places.json"` and the like), and its plan is `docs\plan-house.md`. It
still plays at `https://house.drok-game.pages.dev/` until that address is used for something else.

**What was taken from it into the pirate game:** grounds (biomes) in a place, with the name shown when he walks
into one; monsters that sleep far from him, so an island can be big. **Worth taking when an island is big enough to
want it:** a safe spot inside a place (`home`: nothing comes for him there, the stall gets new pieces when he comes
in), falling without the place starting over, the map window drawn from the rules' layout, and the stand-in
painter for a place with no art (`paintHouse`).
