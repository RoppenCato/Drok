## Ideas (anyone's; marked whose)

All of these are Claude's suggestions from Robin's brainstorm of 2026-11-19. Robin: "not decided: ask me". The number
in brackets is the startup question that asks about it.

1. Target-based combat with auto-attack, not aimed swings. Fits one thumb: the thumb steers and presses skills, the
   hero swings by himself. (6)
2. The Warrior's kit: rage built by hitting, Charge, Cleave or Whirlwind, one defensive cooldown. (7)
3. Carry over from Zite (`RoppenCato/Zite`) the ideas, not the code or the art, of: gear ranks, rarities, affixes,
   uniques and sets, chests and keyed vaults, talent trees. (11)
4. Each monster has one rare signature drop. A monster book that fills in as he hunts, as the unlock layer that is
   kept for good. (12)
5. Uniques that change how a skill works, not only numbers. (12)
6. Level-gated hunting grounds, rare spawns and bosses on timers. (10)
7. Job advancements at set levels. (13)
8. Risky gear upgrading, like scrolls in MapleStory. (13)
9. Loot drops on the ground and is picked up by walking over it. Robin added: "Needs to have coloroed effects for
   rarity." (14)
10. An "export save" button from day one, since the save lives only on the phone. (17)
11. Skills in an arc in the bottom corner; bag, map and character at the top. (17)

Still open from the brainstorm, in Robin's words: "The name, the setting, the look, how gear shows on the hero, and how
the world is laid out." The name and the look's direction he gave the same day (Drok; dark fantasy); the rest are
questions 5, 9, 20 and 21.
