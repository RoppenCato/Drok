# Chapter 1: the plan

The checklist of chapter 1 (`chapters.md`), in the order it is built. Each item: who does it (GD, AD, SD), what it needs
from the studios, its sub-items, **Built** when it works with its tests, **Checked** when Robin has tried it on his
device and said so (with the date). A change to a checked item re-opens it. "What's next" reads this list top down.

Legend: `[ ]` open, `[x]` done. Who: GD, AD, SD. A stand-in holds a place until a drop lands, so nothing here waits.
