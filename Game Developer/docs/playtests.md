# The playtest book

Robin, 2026-11-04 (FapleGlory): a place where the progress, the mistakes and what went well are kept, so the 2D game
guide learns from every game. Every round of Robin's play goes here: his notes word for word, what was found, what was
done, and the rule each left. Newest at the bottom. The rules that serve any game move into `lessons.md` and, at a
chapter's close, into the template (the skill `harvest`). The skill `playtest-check` writes the round here.
