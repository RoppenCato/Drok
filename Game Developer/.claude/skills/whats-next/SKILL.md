# What's next

Read `docs\chapters.md` (the current chapter and its "done when"), `docs\plan-chapter-N.md` (the checklist),
`docs\autonomous.md` (what may be done alone) and `docs\idea-bank.md` (what waits for Robin). Then answer in this form,
nothing more:

1. **Next**: three to five items from the plan, in order, each with who does it (GD, AD, SD) and what it waits on (a
   drop, a decision of Robin's, nothing).
2. **The chapter stands at**: items built, items checked by Robin, items open; what the "done when" still lacks.
3. **Waits for Robin**: the decisions and picks on the list, numbered, so he can answer any by number.

If he then says "do it" or "go", take the first item that needs no decision of his, do it to the end (tests, the pane,
the commit), and come back with the next. If the plan is empty or stale, say so and offer to write the next chapter's
plan (the skill `chapter-plan`).
