# A chapter's plan

Robin, 2026-11-04 (the template): a chapter is a piece of the game that matters, ending in something playable; a
checklist per chapter, broken into sub-items, so "what's next" always has an answer.

1. Read `docs\chapters.md` (the chapter's lines and its "done when"), the brief (what is decided), `docs\idea-bank.md`
   (what is open), the last chapter's plan (what was left), and `docs\state\` for the systems the chapter touches.
2. Write `docs\plan-chapter-N.md` in the form of chapter 1's: numbered items in build order, each with who (GD, AD, SD),
   its sub-items, what it needs from the studios (and the stand-in meanwhile), `Built` and `Checked by Robin` boxes;
   then "Needs from the studios, in order"; then "Done when" copied from `chapters.md`.
3. Add the chapter's waves to `docs\art-demands.md` and `docs\sound-demands.md` with their budgets, and send the
   studios their first requests (the form in `docs\requests.md`). Big work: three concepts; any song: three versions.
4. Mark in `docs\autonomous.md` the items that need no decision of Robin's.
5. Show Robin the plan in the chat as a short list and stop: his yes before item one (his rule: "if he asks for a
   list or plan to confirm, show it and stop"). When he says go, start at item one.

When a chapter **closes** (its "done when" true on Robin's device, the tag on main), before the next plan is written:
run the skill `harvest` (Robin, 2026-11-04): what the chapter taught (`docs\lessons.md`, `docs\playtests.md`, the felt
numbers, the decisions) moves into the template at `C:\dev\2D game template`, and a starter is proposed when the
game's bones are worth one.
